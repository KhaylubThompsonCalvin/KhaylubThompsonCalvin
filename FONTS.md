# Fonts in the banner images

The SVG images in `assets/` embed subsets of two fonts, both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1:

- **Fraunces** (headings): license in `fonts/OFL-Fraunces.txt`.
- **KT Sans** (body text): a subset of **IBM Plex Sans**. "Plex" is a Reserved Font Name under the OFL, so the
  modified version carries its own name. License in `fonts/OFL-IBM-Plex.txt`.

The same faces and colors are used on [khaylub.com](https://khaylub.com).
